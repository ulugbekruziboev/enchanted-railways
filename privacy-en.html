Privacy policy

Valid from 1 November 2026. This policy covers the app Enchanted Railways (Android and web version).

Who is responsible

Ulugbek Ruziboev
Germany
Email: developmentenchanted@gmail.com

In short

The app collects no personal data. It needs no account, shows no ads, contains no tracking, statistics or advertising code, and does not connect to the internet. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

The pieces you own and their counts, any pieces you added yourself, the bridge settings, the language you chose and a few settings (for example whether you have seen the introduction). This is stored only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see or access it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

If you use Export, the app makes a text code of your pieces. It contains only the pieces, nothing about you. It goes only where you copy it to yourself.

Android backup

If you have turned on backup on your Android device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account. Google processes this backup for you under its own terms and privacy policy. We have no access to it.

Feedback by email

The "Send feedback" button opens your own email app. If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use this only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in answering questions about the app; for questions about a purchase, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Our mailbox is with Google (Gmail), so Google processes these emails for us; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. We delete emails when the matter is settled, at the latest after 12 months, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google processes data about the download and your account under its own privacy policy. From Google Play we receive only summary statistics (for example the number of installations) and anonymous technical crash reports, which contain no data that identifies you.

Children

The app is made for families and can be used by children. It collects no data from children. Sending feedback is meant for parents.

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open them, GitHub processes your IP address and technical details of the request in its server logs to deliver the pages and keep the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The pages use no cookies, no tracking and load nothing from other servers.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). Since the app collects nothing, this usually concerns only emails you sent us. Write to the email address above. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your data, we will update this policy before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent where the law requires it.
